About Ziza Holding LLC

By the Community. Forever.

Ziza is a transportation ecosystem headquartered in Atlantic County, New Jersey. Built with a permanent charitable heart, Ziza is committed to giving back to the communities it serves.

A seat for Philanthropy. To reflect its commitment, Ziza reserves a symbolic seat at the members' table for Philanthropy. Each year, the members vote to select the nonprofit organization that will receive the company's annual charitable contribution.

Ziza isn't one company — it's a foundation with businesses growing out of it. Thirteen entities in the ecosystem sit underneath Ziza Holding, organized into four generations to deploy over the years.
